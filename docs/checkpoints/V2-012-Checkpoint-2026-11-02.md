## Security Finding

The employee could access Edit in Grid View.

Administrative fields were visible, including Status,
Approver, Approval Decision, Approval Comments,
Approval Date, Assigned Administrator, Fulfillment Notes,
and Fulfillment Date.

The employee opened the Status dropdown and could select
administrative workflow values, including Approved and Rejected.

No unauthorized status change was saved or verified.

Hiding fields from the New Item form does not enforce
SharePoint field-level security.

## Recommended Remediation

Maintain separate employee intake and administrative lists.

Use Power Automate to validate and transfer submissions
from Employee Intake to the administrator-controlled
Access Requests list.

Ensure employees cannot modify administrative approval
or fulfillment records.
